Engineering workbook · Grades 9–12 · 7 chapters

Basic Engineering

Seven chapters, like a math book. Each chapter has numbered sections (1.1, 1.2, 1.3 and so on). Every topic takes five sections: learn, math, lab, practice, and a check with a design challenge. Math is built into every topic, and the last chapter is your capstone project.

Close answers count. If you get stuck twice, you can see how to solve it. When you finish every problem in a section, answer the quick feedback question and your completion card appears. Screenshot it. At the end of each topic (five sections), put your five screenshots in one document and turn it in on Canvas.
